Characters Guide

Two protagonists, two distinct playstyles. Master both Yasuke and Naoe in Assassin's Creed Shadows.

🥷

Naoe (奈緒江)

The Shinobi • Stealth Specialist

17-year-old Iga ninja, daughter of Fujibayashi Nagato. Master of stealth, shadows, and silent elimination. Perfect for players who prefer precision over power.

Core Abilities

  • • Shadow elimination - silent takedowns
  • • Shuriken throwing - ranged stealth
  • • Wall climbing - enhanced parkour
  • • Smoke bombs - escape and approach

Best For

Stealth Infiltration Silent Kills
⚔️

Yasuke (彌助)

The Samurai • Combat Specialist

Historic African samurai who served Oda Nobunaga. Combat-focused warrior with heavy weapons and armor. Ideal for direct confrontation.

Core Abilities

  • • Heavy combat - stand against armies
  • • Katana mastery - powerful strikes
  • • Armor training - damage resistance
  • • Honor system - stance-based combat

Best For

Combat Boss Fights Melee
