Patience First

  • • Study enemy positions first
  • • Listen for alert sounds

Environmental Use

  • • Use barrels for distraction
  • • Ledges for aerial takedowns
  • • Smoke bombs for escape
  • • Throw coins to lure guards

Yasuke's Strength

  • • Chain attacks for style points

Naoe's Agility
